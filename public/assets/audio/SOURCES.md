# Audio provenance

No AI-generated media is used. These are original 16-second arrangements made with deterministic audio DSP.

- Ceremony: recorded trumpet samples by Versilian Studios, VSCO 2 Community Edition (CC0 1.0). Original fanfare arrangement and room delay.
- Cocktail: original resonant glass-clink model and a quiet chord bed, authored in code.
- Dance floor: original 120 BPM drum, bass, and chord loop, authored in code.

Trumpet source: https://versilian-studios.com/vsco-community/
Repository: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE

Recordings: Brass/Trumpet/sus/Sum_SHTrumpet_sus_C3_v1_rr1.wav; Brass/Trumpet/sus/Sum_SHTrumpet_sus_G3_v1_rr1.wav; Brass/Trumpet/stac/Sum_SHTrumpet_stac_C3_v1_rr1.wav. CC0 license is included alongside these exports.

The arrangements are preview sound design, not recordings of Space Coast Sounds events.
